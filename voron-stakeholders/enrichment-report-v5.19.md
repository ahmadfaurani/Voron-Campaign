## Research Methodology

### Source Strategies Used
1. **ASIFMA Speaker Profiles** — Self-authored executive bios for financial industry leaders (HIGH confidence)
2. **TheOfficialBoard** — Executive directory with verified organizational positions (HIGH confidence)
3. **LinkedIn Profiles** — Professional profiles with title and organization verification (HIGH-MEDIUM confidence)
4. **Cybersecurity Conference Speaker Lists** — Industry event speaker bios (HIGH confidence)
5. **RocketReach** — Data aggregator profiles (MEDIUM confidence, requires further verification)
6. **Firecrawl Agent** — Autonomous web research agent for complex multi-source searches
7. **Official Bank Leadership Pages** — CIMB, HLB, HSBC Malaysia leadership pages scraped (CISOs not listed at executive level)

### Confidence Levels
- **HIGH (85-90):** Multiple independent sources confirm role and institution. Official sources or self-authored bios.
- **MEDIUM-HIGH (70-80):** Single reliable source (LinkedIn, conference bio) with title match.
- **MEDIUM (55-65):** Data aggregator only (RocketReach), not yet verified via LinkedIn or official source.

### Research Challenges
- **CISOs not listed on official leadership pages:** Most Malaysian banks list only Board of Directors and C-suite executives (CEO, CFO, CRO) on their websites. CISOs typically report to CIO/CTO and are not listed at the executive level.
- **Search engine limitations:** Web searches for "{Institution} CISO Malaysia" frequently return irrelevant results (US bank login pages, nursing degrees for "BSN", car drivetrains for "FWD", gaming browsers for "GX Bank").
- **LinkedIn access:** LinkedIn profiles require authentication for full access; many CISO profiles are partially restricted.
- **Digital banks (GX Bank):** Newly established (2024) digital banks may not have finalized their senior management teams publicly.
