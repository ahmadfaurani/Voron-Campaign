## Methodology

### Tools Used
1. **web_extract** — Direct URL extraction from LinkedIn company pages and corporate websites
2. **Firecrawl scrape** — Advanced scraping with stealth/enhanced proxy for anti-bot protected sites
3. **Firecrawl search** — Domain-restricted search for Malaysian news sites
4. **Firecrawl agent** — Autonomous multi-step research agent
5. **web_search** — Supplementary web search (limited effectiveness for Malaysian-specific queries)

### Limitations Encountered
- **Anti-bot protection**: PayNet (paynet.my) blocks all automated access including stealth proxy
- **DNS resolution**: .com.my domains failed via Firecrawl (ipay88.com.my, senangpay.com.my)
- **Search backend**: web_search consistently returned irrelevant results for Malaysian company names (Microsoft, dictionary definitions, Japanese articles)
- **LinkedIn profiles**: Individual LinkedIn profiles return empty content (private/locked profiles)
- **Small fintech companies**: 11-50 employee companies typically don't have formal C-suite roles (CISO, CRO, Compliance, Audit)

### Confidence Scoring
- **90-100**: Official company website leadership page + cross-referenced with news
- **80-89**: Official LinkedIn company page + confirmed via multiple sources
- **70-79**: LinkedIn company page or third-party profile (RocketReach, TheOrg)
- **60-69**: Single source, unverified
- **Below 60**: Speculative or inferred
