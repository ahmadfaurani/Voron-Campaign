## Key Findings

- **CISO is the hardest role to identify publicly**: Only 98/207 institutions (47.3%) publicly disclose their CISO. Most Malaysian financial institutions do not list CISO on their public websites.
- **CFO has the highest coverage**: 152/207 (73.4%) of institutions publicly disclose their CFO.
- **Foreign bank subsidiaries** (BNP Paribas, Mizuho, Bank of America, Deutsche Bank, J.P. Morgan) typically do not publish Malaysia-specific C-suite leadership on public websites. These roles are managed at regional/global level.
- **Insurance/takaful companies** often share leadership at group level (e.g., Allianz, MNRB, Manulife) — individual subsidiary CISO/CRO/CCO roles may not be publicly listed.
- **PayNet products** (DuitNow, FPX, JomPAY, Me2U, PayDirect, PayNet Card) inherit leadership from PayNet parent — 6 of 7 roles are inherited for each product entity.
