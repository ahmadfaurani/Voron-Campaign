## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The 16th Johor State Election (PRN Johor) concluded peacefully on 11 July 2026 with Barisan Nasional retaining power with 48 of 56 seats. PDRM deployed over 14,000 personnel (including 5,600 from out-of-state contingents). 90 police reports were lodged and 25 investigation papers opened during the 14-day campaign — none implicating candidates or senior party leaders. No major security incidents occurred on polling day. Post-election, PDRM leadership has pivoted to broader strategic security themes: AI/digitalisation of policing (DIGP Ayob Khan, 16 July) and inter-agency coordination on hybrid threats and cross-border crime (JPGK Ke-81, 17 July).
