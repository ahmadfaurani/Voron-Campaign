## KDN/MOHA & NATIONAL SECURITY LEGISLATION

### Communications and Multimedia (Amendment) Bill 2026 — PASSED 15 July 2026
- Passed by Dewan Rakyat; presented by Deputy Communications Minister **Teo Nie Ching**.
- Amends Act 588 (Comm. & Multimedia Act 1998), Section 202 — introduces new subsections 202(1A), 202(1B), amends 202(2).
- **National security relevance:** Creates legal basis for a "National Universal Service Provision (USP) Initiative" allowing the Minister to direct MCMC to support national-security-related comms initiatives (network facilities/services installation).
- **Check & balance:** National security matters must be determined by the **National Security Council under Act 776 (NSC Act 2016)**.
- Rationale: cybersecurity threats, geopolitical tensions, risks of service disruptions / data leaks / disruption to critical systems.
- Sources: Malay Mail (15 July), NST (passed with security safeguards).
